# Accessibility statement

A11yScanFix includes an accessibility statement generator - free, on purpose.
Many sites are legally encouraged to publish one.

## Generate a statement

1. Go to **A11yScanFix -> Accessibility Statement** (or the relevant settings
   tab).
2. Fill in your organisation details and the standard you target.
3. Generate the statement page.

## Publish it

- A dedicated statement page is created.
- Optionally add a link in your site footer.
- Use the shortcode to place the statement anywhere.
