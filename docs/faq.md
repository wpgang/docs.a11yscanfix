# FAQ

## Why do some pages fail during a scan?

A page can time out or fail to save its results (a slow server, a heavy page, a
momentary glitch). Those pages are listed in the scan log and can be re-run with
**Retry failed pages**.

## Do I need to keep the tab open during a full-site scan?

Yes. The scan runs in your browser tab. Leaving the page cancels it; use
**Pause** if you need to stop and continue later.

## Why does another tool show a different number of issues?

Tools use different rule sets and group issues differently. A11yScanFix uses
axe-core and counts one rule failure as one issue. Your enabled standards and
ignored rules also change the total.

## Why doesn't "Issues Found" drop the moment I turn on fixers?

Fixers change your live site, but the dashboard reflects the last **scan**.
Re-scan to see the new numbers.

## Does scanning send my data anywhere?

No. Scanning runs entirely in your browser. AI alt text is the only feature that
contacts an outside service - the AI provider you chose, with your own key.

## Can it fix everything automatically?

No. Automated fixes handle common, well-defined problems. Some issues (like
colour contrast choices or meaningful content order) need human judgement.
