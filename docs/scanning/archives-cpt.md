# Custom post types, archives and taxonomies <span class="pro">PRO</span>

!!! note "PRO feature"
    Free scans posts and pages only. Pro widens coverage to the rest of your
    site.

## Custom post types

Pro lets you include any registered custom post type in the full-site scan
(for example WooCommerce products). Choose which types to scan in the settings.

## Archives and taxonomies

Archive and taxonomy pages (category, tag, custom taxonomy, and WooCommerce
product categories / tags) have no single post record, so they are scanned and
stored separately from per-post results, with their own block on the dashboard.

You can scan a **Sample** (a few representative pages) or **All** of them.
