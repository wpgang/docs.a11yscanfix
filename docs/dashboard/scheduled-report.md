# Scheduled email report <span class="pro">PRO</span>

!!! note "PRO feature"
    The scheduled email report is part of the A11yScanFix Pro add-on.

Get a branded accessibility summary in your inbox on a schedule, so you (or your
client) see progress without logging in.

## Settings

- **Frequency** - Off / Weekly / Monthly.
- **Recipients** - one or more email addresses (defaults to the admin email).

## What the email shows

The report mirrors the dashboard: open issues, severity split, pages scanned,
passed-checks percentage, top problematic pages, and the most common issues,
with change arrows versus the previous report.

If the last scan was cancelled, the email shows a clear note that the figures
are partial; a completed scan shows the scan date in green.
